About CK Cake Lounge

Owner Image Placeholder

Welcome to CK Cake Lounge, where we believe that everyone deserves to enjoy extraordinary baked goods, no matter their dietary choices or restrictions.

"The culmination of a long-standing baking hobby, CK Cake lounge was formally established in 2020. We took that personal dedication and commitment to flavor, elevated it to a professional standard, and continued to serve delicious, high-quality eggless goods to this day."

Our journey began with a simple mission: to prove that you don't need eggs to create a cake that's moist, a cookie that's chewy, or a pastry that's perfectly flaky. We spent years perfecting recipes, focusing on premium, quality ingredients and innovative techniques to deliver treats that are consistently superior.

What makes us special?

  • 100% Eggless: Perfect for vegans, those with egg allergies, or anyone seeking ethical alternatives.
  • Uncompromising Flavor: Forget "just okay." Our focus is on decadent, unforgettable taste.
  • Baked with Love: Every item is made from scratch daily, using the finest natural ingredients.

From our kitchen to your table, we are here to celebrate life’s moments with delicious, inclusive sweetness. We can't wait for you to taste the difference!

Parminder Verma

Founder, CK Cake Lounge
